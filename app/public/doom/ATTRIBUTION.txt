doom-v0.1.0-sfx.wasm
====================

A build of doom.wasm with sound effects added:

    https://github.com/jacobenget/doom.wasm
    tag: v0.1.0

The changes to that source and the script that builds this file are in tools/doom of this repository:

    https://github.com/unremarkablegarden/cyberspace-terminal

    tools/doom/i_wasm_sound.c   sound effects module (new file)
    tools/doom/sound.patch      registers the module in doomgeneric/src/i_sound.c
    tools/doom/build.sh         fetches the tag, applies both, compiles and links

Upstream's source at the tag plus those three files is the complete corresponding source of this binary.
